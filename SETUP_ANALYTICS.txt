TO UPDATE YOUR EXISTING GITHUB PAGES SITE
1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. Open https://github.com/thindpls/daily-christian-prayer
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload ONLY index.html, analytics.js, and sw.js from inside the unzipped folder.
   Allow GitHub to replace existing files with the same names.
5. Commit changes. Do not upload the ZIP itself or change the other files.
6. Open https://thindpls.github.io/daily-christian-prayer/ while online.
7. Visit your GoatCounter dashboard after some visits to check page views and events.

MEASUREMENTS
Standard page views: online site openings.
prayer-open: prayer displayed after page open or name setup.
first-prayer-use: first detected prayer use in that browser.
return-day: prayer displayed on a later date in the same browser.
home-screen-launch: app opened in standalone mode, not proof of installation.
paypal-link-click: click on PayPal support link, not confirmed payment.

LIMITATIONS
There is no reliable exact count of installs or distinct people. Offline opens and
privacy-blocked tracking may be missing. Names, contact details and payment
information are not sent to GoatCounter by this integration. Tracking starts
after the upload is published; past site visits cannot be reconstructed.
