Policy prepared November 27, 2026. Describes this version of the application.

Daily Christian Prayer is free to use without an account, email signup or required payment. This page explains how the website handles information.

Information saved on your device

The app uses browser local storage to remember your optional first name, name-display preference, whether setup is complete, whether you dismissed the installation reminder, and the last recorded day of prayer use. The service worker can also store website files in your browser cache for offline access.

Our application code does not include your name or personalized prayer text in analytics events or the built-in sharing messages. If you choose to share information yourself, the receiving service may handle that information under its own policies.

Usage analytics

The main prayer page uses GoatCounter when online. The analytics script can record page visits, referral and campaign information, browser and device characteristics, approximate country and other usage information. Custom events measure prayer opens, first and returning prayer use, home-screen launches, browser-reported installations where supported, sharing and copying actions, friend invitations and clicks on the primary PayPal donation link. These events use predefined names rather than your saved name or prayer text.

GoatCounter is designed to operate without tracking cookies. Our own application uses local storage rather than setting cookies. External services may use their own cookies or other storage. See GoatCounter's privacy information.

Analytics choice

You can use the GoatCounter tracking toggle on the main prayer page to change tracking for your current browser. Follow the confirmation message to determine whether tracking is enabled or disabled. Browser privacy controls or content blockers may also prevent analytics. Clearing site storage may reset the choice.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like other web hosting services, GitHub may process network and request information when you access the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Donations are optional and handled by PayPal. The prayer app does not collect payment details. PayPal and Facebook, and any other services you choose to open or use for sharing, operate under their own privacy policies.

Offline access

Daily prayers may be available offline after successful caching. Online services, updates, external sharing and donations require internet access.

Clear saved information

Use Reset in Prayer Settings to clear your saved name, display preference, setup state and installation reminder preference. To remove all site data, including the analytics last-use marker and offline cache, use your browser's site-data controls. This may also remove your offline access and tracking choice. Clearing local data does not erase previously recorded analytics.

Contact and updates

If you contact us by email, your email address and message are received so we can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. For privacy questions, use the email button on our Contact page. We will update this policy when relevant features or practices change.
