Our mission

Daily Christian Prayer encourages a meaningful daily relationship with God through prayer, Scripture reading and personal reflection. Our mission is simple: A Moment With God, Every Day.

About the founder

Rev. Tim Helmick Sr.
Founder and Creator of Daily Christian Prayer

Rev. Tim Helmick Sr. created Daily Christian Prayer to help individuals and families make time for God each day, and to make Christian prayer accessible regardless of location, financial circumstances or technical experience.

What we offer

Our free resource includes 366 daily prayers with Bible references, optional first-name personalization, and Advent and Christmas prayers. No account or subscription is required. Donations are entirely voluntary.
